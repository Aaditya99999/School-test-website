From the Principal's Desk

Every child arrives with something worth drawing out.

Our work is not to fill a syllabus but to find what each student is genuinely curious about, and then give them the discipline to pursue it.

We cap our classrooms at 28 so no child becomes a face in a crowd, and every teacher holds a daily open hour. We have held a 100% board pass rate for eleven years — but the number I care most about is the one we never publish: 340 alumni came back to visit their teachers last year.
