Welcome to

Radha Krishna Gurukulam

An ICSE school in Kanpur, from a kindergarten section through classes 9 to 12, with dedicated preparation for IIT-JEE, NEET and NDA running alongside the school years. Students who live away from home can stay in our hostel, with meals from the mess.

We teach with experienced faculty, keep a close eye on progress through regular tests, and give every student a mentor. Discipline and values are part of the day, not an afterthought.
